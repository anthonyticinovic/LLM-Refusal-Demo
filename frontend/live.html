What you're looking at

Negative α ablates. At every block, the residual stream has its component along r̂ removed: h ← h − t(h·r̂)r̂. Nothing about the prompt changes — only the model's ability to represent this one direction.

Positive α injects at the extraction layer alone: h ← h + α·r̂. Try it on a prompt about bread, or tides. The refusals it produces are often visibly confused about what they are refusing, which is the tell that this is a direction being forced rather than a judgement being made.

The direction was never told about these prompts. It is a difference-of-means over a separate extraction set. Held-out prompts are the only ones worth judging it on.
